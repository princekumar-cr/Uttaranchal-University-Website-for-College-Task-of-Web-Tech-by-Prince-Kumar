About Uttaranchal University

🏛️ Our History

Uttaranchal University was established in 2013 under the Uttaranchal University Act, 2012 and is approved by the UGC. It began as a small seat of learning and has rapidly grown into one of North India's most dynamic private universities. Its roots, however, go back to 1996 through the legacy of its parent organisations - the Highways Institute of Management and the S.M.S. Group of Institutions, pioneers in professional education in Uttarakhand.

Today the university runs 75+ programmes across its faculties, nurtures students from all corners of the country, and is proudly recognised as a NAAC 'A+' accredited university.

🎯 Our Vision

To be a centre of excellence that imparts globally relevant education, fosters research and innovation, and transforms young minds into competent, humane and socially responsible professionals.

🚀 Our Mission

To provide quality, affordable education through an industry-aligned curriculum; to create state-of-the-art learning infrastructure; and to develop ethical, skilled graduates who contribute positively to society and the economy.
